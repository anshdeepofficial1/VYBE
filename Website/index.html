Music, from discovery to playback

Everything you listen to

VYBE brings online discovery, your local library, downloads and playback together in a UI based on the same rounded Material system as the Android app.

01 / Discover

Discover new music

Explore latest releases, Quick Picks, recommendations, songs, albums and artists through the YouTube Music catalog.

02 / Library

Your library

Liked music, local songs, playlists, albums, artists, folders and downloads in one place.

03 / Offline

Listen offline

Download supported tracks and keep track of download progress inside the app.

04 / Lyrics

Follow the lyrics

Automatic synchronized lyrics, current-line following and optional AI-powered editing, translation or romanization with your own configured provider.
